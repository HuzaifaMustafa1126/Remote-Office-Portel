# Phase 1.1 requirements

Phase 1.1 delivers a secure project and database foundation: email/password authentication, JWT sessions, employee creation and non-destructive activation status, permission-based roles, audit history, organization totals, and a responsive desktop/mobile layout.

The system intentionally excludes attendance, clock/break tracking, leave, salary, payroll, and reporting business rules. Their folders are placeholders only. Employee IDs are stable and employees are deactivated rather than deleted so later historical records retain referential integrity.
